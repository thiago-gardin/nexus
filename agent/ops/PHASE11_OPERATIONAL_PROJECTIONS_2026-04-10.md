# Phase 11 Boundary Note

## In Scope

- Domain operational summary projections
- Pending review queue projections
- Publication of those projections into `tree/domains/<domain_id>/projections/`
- Navigation from human and agent surfaces to the published projections

## Out of Scope

- Graph UI
- Cockpit rich
- MCP complete
- Reconcilation / canonicalization
- Advanced automation beyond read-only projection navigation
- New mutation paths

## Notes

The projections must remain derived from executable SQLite-backed state and remain non-sovereign publication artifacts.
